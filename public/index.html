Live CONFORMIST system prompt

The outer loop improves the CONFORMIST profile's system prompt only, to keep scope tight. EXPLORER and SPRINTER prompts are fixed.

1 · Maker proposal

The Maker sees the live prompt, this session's training scores, and the 3 lowest-scoring cases only — never gate internals, held-out cases, or eval scoring code.

2 · Checker — candidate vs. live, same session

Comparing the candidate's fresh runs against a stale recorded baseline is not a valid comparison; the baseline is measured under the same settings, in this same run.

3 · Decision — gates plus target

4 · Held-out commit run

A training gain without a held-out gain is overfitting, measured.

5 · Promotion gate

The loop has autonomy over exploration and zero autonomy over promotion. Only a human Approve makes a candidate live.

6 · History — append-only
